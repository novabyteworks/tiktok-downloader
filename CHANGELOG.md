## 1.0.3 · November 2026

- **My Library**: everything you saved in one gallery, with collections, favourites, tags, duplicates, two pictures side by side and a timed slideshow. Open it from the panel's footer.
- **Smart search** in My Library, on paid plans. It runs on your own computer.
- If you also use our Instagram or multi-site downloader, their pictures show up in the same gallery
